Privacy Policy

FileExplorer for Android — Last updated: August 17, 2026

Overview

FileExplorer is a file management app developed by Doppelops LC. This policy describes what data the app accesses, how it is used, and your choices.

Data We Access

FileExplorer accesses files and folders on your device solely to provide file management functionality. All file operations happen locally on your device. We do not upload, copy, or transmit your files to any server unless you explicitly use a sharing feature (WiFi Direct, LAN file sharing, or cloud storage).

Permissions

  • Storage access — Required to browse, copy, move, rename, and delete files on your device.
  • Nearby devices / WiFi — Used only when you initiate WiFi Direct file transfers. No location data is collected.
  • Internet — Used for Google Drive integration (when you connect your account), displaying ads, and LAN file sharing.
  • Install packages — Allows you to install APK files you select. No packages are installed without your explicit action.
  • Notifications — Used to show progress for background file operations and LAN server status.
  • Query installed apps — Used to display app information and manage APK files.

Google Drive

If you choose to connect your Google account, FileExplorer accesses your Google Drive files using OAuth 2.0 authentication. Your Google credentials are never seen or stored by the app; authentication is handled entirely by Google. OAuth tokens are stored in encrypted local storage on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.

Advertising

FileExplorer displays ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and interaction data to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device’s Google settings. You may also purchase a premium subscription to remove all ads.

For more information, see Google’s Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are processed through Google Play and managed by RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous user identifier and purchase data from Google Play. No personal information beyond what Google Play provides is shared with RevenueCat.

See RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy.

LAN File Sharing

The built-in HTTP and FTP servers operate only on your local network and only while you have them running. Files are served directly from your device to other devices on the same network. No data passes through external servers.

Data Collection Summary

  • We do not collect personal information, analytics, or usage data.
  • We do not have servers that receive your data.
  • AdMob (Google) collects advertising-related data as described above.
  • RevenueCat processes subscription data as described above.
  • Google Drive tokens are stored locally in encrypted storage.

Data Security

Sensitive data such as OAuth tokens and network credentials are stored using Android’s EncryptedSharedPreferences, backed by the Android Keystore system. No sensitive data is stored in plaintext.

Children’s Privacy

FileExplorer is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.
